The Four Social Styles

Every style has real strengths — the assessment simply reveals your natural tendencies.

Analytical

Logical, careful, and data-driven. Prefers facts and organized thinking over gut instinct.

Driver

Direct, decisive, and results-focused. Moves quickly and takes charge to get things done.

Amiable

Friendly, supportive, and relationship-oriented. Values cooperation and harmony.

Expressive

Energetic, enthusiastic, and persuasive. Thrives on people, ideas, and spontaneity.
